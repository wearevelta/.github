## Velta — Security & Compliance

**From first audit to continuous compliance.**

Velta is an independent GRC and audit readiness advisory practice helping B2B technology companies prepare for, achieve, and maintain rigorous security and compliance standards without operational friction.

### Core Focus Areas
- **Audit Readiness & Remediation:** SOC 1, SOC 2 (Type I & Type II), ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management Systems)
- **Continuous GRC & Governance:** Control mapping, evidence automation workflows, ITGC, and Third-Party Vendor Risk Management (TPRM)
- **Security Posture & Trust:** Executive advisory, policy architecture, and security posture alignment
